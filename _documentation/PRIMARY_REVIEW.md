## What the stronger account claims

MORARG defends a constitutive claim about human interpersonal morality. A standard is valid through adequate justification to affected people under defended conditions of standing, reciprocity, information and freedom from coercion. Actual endorsement does not make it valid. An unheard complaint can defeat an accepted rule; the relevant question is whether its justification survives, not whether its critic wins.

The positive argument concerns the authority a demand claims over another life. Preference explains why its author wants compliance; power explains how compliance may be obtained. Neither answers the affected person's claim. We defend reciprocal answerability because comparable interests require a relevant distinction, and the authority asserted over another person cannot be justified solely by the identity or power of its claimant. Needs, responsibilities and circumstances can supply relevant distinctions. This is substantive moral reasoning, not a deduction of equal standing from self-interest.

The further inference is **sufficiency**: properly justified relations constitute the authority of interpersonal demands. The positive case connects what is owed, who may demand it, who is owed repair and how a neglected complaint can correct an accepted rule. These relations have explanatory fit; the account does not establish that a strong relational realist explains them less well. The preference for grounding authority in those relations is a defended normative-explanatory commitment. A realist may accept every practical reason and relation while saying independent normative truths explain their authority. They need no additional first-order verdict or complaint. The full numbered argument and its failure conditions are in [AUTHORITY_ARGUMENT.md](AUTHORITY_ARGUMENT.md).

This account does not purport to construct every practical reason from a normatively empty starting point. Equal standing and limits on imposed burdens receive an interpersonal defence, but their authority is not demonstrated to exclude every independent explanation. Local construction can coexist with realism about those starting reasons. MORARG therefore preserves its affirmative constitutive thesis while leaving the broader antirealist ambition unproved. A person can also discover what a constructed relation requires; the dispute concerns grounding, not whether learning an answer is possible.

## The strongest remaining objections

**Prior reasons and sufficiency.** The account gives a positive relational explanation, but its fit to accountability does not by itself establish superiority over a realist who accepts the same relation. Scanlon's combination of interpersonal construction with underived reasons makes this a concrete rival. Enoch's global challenge reinforces the need to identify which level is constructed. Railton remains a separate naturalist countermodel: informed interests and human relationships can be objective without present approval creating them.

**Adequacy without immunity.** Facts, alternatives, meaningful challenge, representation and burden comparisons are stated before the verdict. They still require moral judgments of relevance and weight. A hypothetical inclusive inquiry that sacrifices one person's serious bodily interest for many trivial conveniences challenges the substantive weighting, rather than becoming acceptable just because discussion occurred. A credible wrong surviving the stated conditions must be able to force revision of their sufficiency or scope; it cannot automatically be renamed an undiscovered procedural defect.

**Evaluative language and conviction.** A subjective act of judging does not entail that truth depends on the judge's approval. Instrumental success, welfare and moral worth are distinguished in the opening. MORARG's positive premise is that a moral demand governing another person must answer their comparable claim; that premise is defended, not read from the word “good.” Conviction alone neither validates a judgment nor refutes objectivity, and emotion remains a possible source of relevant attention.

**The powerful refuser.** The victim's claim need not disappear when an aggressor refuses it, and others can have reasons to resist. But explaining that claim does not establish that every possible agent has accepted its authority. The exhibit rejects neither the victim's standing nor the objection: it defends the former and identifies the latter's remaining burden. Failure to motivate and absence of a reason are different propositions.

**Circularity and rival starting points.** A procedure does not earn authority by being labelled fair. Wide scrutiny compares rival principles, accurate evidence, difficult cases and excluded perspectives; a protected premise must state what could count against it. This earns more warrant than a repeated assertion, but coherence can also stabilise domination. The branch comparing equality and hereditary hierarchy consequently asks which substantive premise distinguishes them instead of announcing that coherence has done so.

**Representation.** Support the person's own communication where possible; separate known wishes, welfare evidence and prospective interests; disclose proxy conflicts; enable independent challenges; and revise when the person can answer. These are authored safeguards grounded in the stated agency/protection commitments. They are not a fictional claim of consent or a complete answer to conflicting proxies, future identity or nonhuman moral standing.

**Competing justified claims.** The proposed discipline compares alternatives including delay, severity, probability, reversibility, agency and special responsibilities. It gives a defeasible presumption against sacrificing a grave interest for minor convenience. Neither these criteria nor a count of affected people mechanically settles every case. A legitimate decision procedure may select action while leaving a losing complaint morally significant.

## Empirical method review and its boundary

The parent/Terra research extended the evolved-fairness module; see [the maintained research note](EVOLVED_FAIRNESS_RESEARCH.md). It adds selected methods/results from the 2025 bonobo study and a fuller primary-text reading of the 2026 chimpanzee work. The bonobo delivery/partner manipulations complicate a single disappointment explanation; the chimpanzee findings retain food-quality, relationship and alternative-explanation limits. These are bounded interpretation improvements, not an independent reanalysis by Astra.

The 2024 pooled accept/reject analysis, 2025 capuchin group study, September 2026 intermittent-inequity study and human developmental studies retain their distinct tasks and access descriptions. The capuchin and September study full-method access remains limited. The later paper's linked data repository returned 404 in the parent review; that is a failed retrieval, not evidence that data never existed. No primate or infant result establishes moral concepts, universal innate content or constitutive authority.

The Tuskegee, Norway and ozone records retain the established historical, causal-design and projection boundaries. The case continuations use explicitly stipulated facts. No Norway estimate supplies the extra-year scenario and no ozone assessment supplies the hypothetical household floor or deadline. The two new authority diagnostics likewise supply philosophical hypotheses, not additional empirical findings.
