## Judgment

**Category 2: related and promising partners with a deliberate bridge.** They are natural siblings in subject matter and potentially in a product family; they are not already interchangeable layers of one validated moral system. A companion experience needs modest linking and meaningful conceptual/editorial work. Making Topology a quantified implementation or validation of MORARG would require a substantially different and much less defensible claim.

MORARG asks what makes a moral claim justified and, more strongly, what constitutes interpersonal moral authority. Topology asks how a stipulated configuration of judgment changes under social and psychological pressures. Explaining how a judgment arises and defending whether it is justified are connected tasks, but neither substitutes for the other.

Both have real explanatory structure. MORARG exposes premises, rivals, evidence limits and revision. MTOP exposes mechanisms, assumptions and paired counterfactuals; its guided comparison holds starts, traits and random draws constant. Neither currently establishes empirical effectiveness with visitors. MTOP's shared register records an earlier local preview, but this assessment does not verify a currently running deployment.

## Why the partnership is useful

1. MORARG can supply the question that Topology does not compute: what reasons are owed to whom? Topology can make external pressure, temptation, conformity and changes of attention discussable without treating every change of mind as reasoned improvement.
2. The distinction between conviction and warrant fits both. MTOP has an illustrative felt-certainty index; MORARG explicitly separates certainty from justification. The index could illustrate an assumption to challenge, never diagnose a visitor or refute a view.
3. The tools can expose different failure modes. A convincing argument may be difficult to act on under pressure. A stable or convergent population may still exclude a claimant. Neither numerical stability nor actual agreement establishes reciprocal justification.
4. MTOP is not simply designed to reward reflection: its deliberation force shifts scope differently under high personal stakes, and it includes intuitive rescuers. These offer useful challenges to simplistic reason-good/emotion-bad teaching, provided the mechanisms remain labeled as assumptions.

## Adjustments that matter

### Binding is not justificatory authority

MTOP y is the extent to which sanction or internal conviction holds a norm in place. MORARG authority concerns a justified claim someone may address to another. Internalized prejudice can be strongly binding psychologically; a justified obligation can be weakly motivating. A shared interface should call this **motivational binding** and preserve a separate account of normative warrant. Do not map MORARG's authority answers to y.

### Scope and reflection are not adequacy scores

MTOP x compresses self, kin, group, society, humanity and sentience onto one line. MORARG needs identifiable affected parties, comparable claims, justified differences, representation and burden distribution. A scalar breadth of concern cannot say which person was excluded or whether a special obligation is justified. MTOP z records a modeled processing style, not quality of inference. Add qualitative affected-party and reason records to a shared case; do not equate positive coordinates with moral progress.

### Values are not one opposition

MTOP w places care/fairness against loyalty/authority/sanctity. Its specification acknowledges that this cannot represent high emphasis on both or low emphasis on both. MORARG's independently selectable values cannot be faithfully reduced to w. Preserve them as separate qualitative commitments. A richer vector would be an optional future model redesign, unnecessary for a simple companion link.

### The model cannot settle realism

The actual MTOP interface already states that moral realism does not entail a physical pull toward a point and that its experiments cannot establish realism's truth, falsity or redundancy. Preserve this. Its code still contains an `objective` force and arbitrarily located theory markers. A shared product should consistently describe these as fixed-reference sensitivity experiments, avoid presenting theory coordinates as diagnoses, and never use convergence or explanatory self-containment as evidence for MORARG's constitutive thesis. MORARG explicitly permits a realist to agree with the same practical safeguards while disputing their ground.

### Tighten residual behavioral and evidential language

MTOP's general methodological framing is careful, but some local copy is stronger. The full readout displays octant “Typical acts”; the Milgram case says obedience weakens although no obedience outcome is computed; the whistleblower case calls its strain a classic moral-injury configuration while the implemented output is an illustrative commitment/pressure index. The map introduction calls its coordinates the “full structure” of judgment, despite omitted context. These are presentation mismatches, not evidence that the named historical claims are false. Before pairing, rewrite these as illustrative associations and distinguish what a cited study reports from what this model computes. This pass did not audit the inherited literature tags or validate its coefficients.

## Recommended pairing

Keep the applications independently usable, under a shared family with two clear invitations: **examine reasons** and **explore pressures**. Preserve MTOP's full vector-field opening; its register explicitly records that the Operator preferred this to a guided-first opening. MORARG can link into a particular secondary guided exercise without changing MTOP's independent default.

A first shared hypothetical case could concern a competent adult offered risky research under financial and institutional pressure:

1. MORARG records affected parties, understanding, alternatives, reasons, objections and a provisional verdict.
2. MTOP explores an explicitly authored change in scarcity or authority pressure while holding other settings fixed. It shows model changes, not a prediction of that participant's consent or behavior.
3. Return to MORARG: did only motivational pressure change, or did the hypothetical also change options and reasons relevant to validity? Have the affected person's objections been answered? A changed coordinate never resolves that question automatically.

The case needs a stated common factual baseline and named counterfactual change. Do not silently replace historical research facts with a simulator's invented inputs.

For a small pilot, manual links and a common case sheet are sufficient. A later transfer contract could include case ID/revision, source application/version, ts_ms, factual stipulations, affected-party IDs, user-authored reasons, model assumptions/settings/seed and outputs. Keep observations, normative commitments, illustrative parameters and model results explicitly distinct. Both apps export JSON, but neither currently supplies an interoperable bridge. MORARG's current/stale/inactive answer contexts must survive any transfer; consent to export does not imply background transmission. Never infer MTOP traits or coordinates from answer flags, completion, selected values or worldview labels.

Technical linking is modest; automatic synchronization or a single runtime is a larger implementation. Python and browser rendering stacks differ but do not prevent sibling tools. The first design question is semantic correctness, not framework replacement.
