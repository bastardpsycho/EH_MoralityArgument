    A meeting is a geometric witness under these authored assumptions. It does not establish agreement about policy, facts, or what anyone should do. The model's one claim is conditional: if real-life factors move people roughly like this, two people can arrive at the same point for different reasons. Removing the factors that only push shows where their points would be without them; what they would then decide is the story's business, not the chart's.
