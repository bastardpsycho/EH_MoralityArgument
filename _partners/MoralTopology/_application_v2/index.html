A meeting is a geometric witness under these authored assumptions. It does not establish agreement about policy, facts, or what anyone should do. The model's one claim is conditional: if influences work roughly like this, then agreement reached through reasons can be told apart from agreement produced by pressure, by asking where it lands and who had to move.
