# Final independent authority critique

Review time: `ts_ms=1793125263000`. Scope: `AUTHORITY_ARGUMENT.md`, `PRIMARY_REVIEW.md`, and the current hub/climate/commons/hall/charter copy in `_application/data.mjs`. No application files changed.

## Finding

The new argument is fairer and stronger. It no longer treats interpersonal answerability as proof of constructivism: it distinguishes the relational question of who may demand what from the grounding question of what makes that demand valid. It owns equal standing, presents adequacy conditions, allows credible counterexamples to defeat sufficiency, limits the thesis to local interpersonal construction, and grants that a realist can accept all the same practical reasons and relations. This supports a substantive intersubjective case; it does not yet establish that reciprocal justification is the truthmaker or refute realism about prior reasons.

P4’s “unification” is genuine explanatory fit: a justified relation links the content of a demand with who can claim it, why a complaint matters, and what correction or repair involves. P5 has not yet shown a comparative advantage over a relational realist who accepts all those facts and explains the relation’s authority through independent normative truths. Saying the account “locates authority” in the relation states the proposed ground; it does not independently show that ground is better. That is acceptable if expressed as a reasoned preference or interpretive commitment. If the copy calls it an abductive result, it should name a feature of relational authority that the realist fails to explain and defend that comparison. It should not require the realist to add a new first-order objection: the rival can keep the same practical verdicts and disagree about their ground.

The source support is well bounded. Darwall’s author-hosted manuscript distinguishes outcome-directed reasons from second-personal claims and treats authority, valid demand, responsibility-to-someone and second-personal reason as interdefined (2009, pp. 1–2). That supports the relational explanandum; interdefinition is not a proof of reciprocal-construction or equal standing. Forst offers a substantial positive model of equal normative authority, but the 2016 article is about basic rights of recognized members of legal-political orders and grounds them in a basic moral right to justification (pp. 1–4). This is a substantive starting point, not a premise-free derivation of a universal moral truthmaker. Railton explicitly allows objective-but-relational moral properties and an agent who lacks a reason to obey while remaining subject to moral standards (1986, pp. 164–165), so neither relationality nor an unmoved refuser selects constructivism on its own.

The adequacy conditions are useful and nontrivial, but “materially affected,” “comparable,” “relevant distinction,” “serious,” and “adequate answer” depend on normative judgments about standing, agency, burdens and weight. The draft acknowledges that; the visitor copy should say these are defended and revisable inputs rather than neutral procedure outputs. Its strongest failure test is a credible wrong that survives every stated condition. Make that operational with one case—for example, whether an informed adult can authorize permanent subordination for a large public benefit. If prohibited, name and defend the future-agency or standing limit; if permitted, explain why present agency outweighs future challenge rights. Do not resolve either answer merely by relabeling the process inadequate.

## Decision-critical visitor-copy wording

The current on-disk `objective-test` says: “A further verdict must offer a relevant reason, rather than merely announce another foundation.” This invites an unfair realist reading. A realist can explain why the same claims count through independent normative truths without adding a further first-order complaint. The current copy also says the interpersonal premise “supports treating justification as constitutive”; it motivates that proposal but does not entail its sufficiency.

Suggested replacement:

> “The realist may accept every complaint and practical reason in this inquiry, while saying independent normative truths explain why they count. Our further claim is that adequate reciprocal justification is sufficient for interpersonal validity. That is a disputed grounding premise: the cases motivate it, but do not entail it.”
